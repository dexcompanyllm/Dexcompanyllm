# Playbook — Multi-AI Workflow

1. Use the latest approved `main` as shared context.
2. Assign one accountable lead.
3. Route subtasks by verified capability, not brand loyalty.
4. Parallelize independent analysis; serialize conflicting writes.
5. Require a canonical result and evidence.
6. Use another run/provider as independent reviewer when risk justifies it.
7. Feed verified reusable lessons back into skills or playbooks.
8. Keep provider-specific optimizations in thin provider entry points.

Do not require every AI to participate in every task.

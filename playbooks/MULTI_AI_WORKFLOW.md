# Playbook — Multi-AI / Multi-Agent Workflow

This workflow applies to current and future AI providers, models, agents, runtimes, and tool-enabled workers.

1. Use latest approved `main` as shared context.
2. Assign one accountable lead.
3. Discover actual capabilities at runtime.
4. Route subtasks by capability, risk, availability, and policy—not brand loyalty.
5. Parallelize independent analysis; serialize conflicting writes.
6. Require one canonical result and evidence.
7. Use an independent worker or run for verification when risk warrants it.
8. Feed verified reusable lessons back into skills or playbooks.
9. Keep vendor/model optimization in optional thin adapters.

Do not require every available AI to participate in every task. Do not hard-code the canonical architecture to today's providers or model names.

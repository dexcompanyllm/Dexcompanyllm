# Skill — Schedule Planning

Status: draft awaiting approval

Plan milestones from evidence, dependencies, ownership, and acceptance criteria. Follow `AI_INSTRUCTIONS.md`; use `skills/validation-gates/SKILL.md` and `skills/human-review/SKILL.md` for controls.

## Evidence priority
For schedule facts, prefer:
1. latest direct statement from the accountable authority
2. confirmed official assignment/contract/role record
3. older plan or roadmap

A lower-priority source must not silently overwrite a newer higher-priority source. When sources conflict, record both source dates and mark `confirmation required`.

Label each schedule statement `confirmed`, `inference`, `proposal`, or `unknown`.

## Procedure
1. Define milestone outcome and scope.
2. Capture source, source date, evidence label, and the people/team the date actually applies to.
3. Verify calendar date/day, working-day or holiday constraints when relevant, and person/team start/end boundaries.
4. Capture prerequisites: access, tools, inbound material, decisions, lead time, and communication channel readiness.
5. Map dependencies and downstream waiters. Add an early intermediate deliverable or quick win when downstream work would otherwise idle.
6. For external coordination, identify the other organization's cadence and owner. Do not describe another organization's cycle as ours.
7. Mark conditional dates explicitly with condition, owner, and fallback/replan trigger.
8. Define approver, decision/review venue, and PASS criteria for each milestone that requires agreement.
9. Check milestone load: flag dates that concentrate multiple major deliverables, decisions, or reviews without evidence of capacity.
10. Set the plan state to `draft awaiting approval` until Owner approval.
11. After every review edit, re-check each changed field against its source and affected dependencies.
12. Start novel delegation with a narrow pilot; expand only after repeated verified success.

## Planning horizon
Use rolling-wave detail: near-term work is detailed; later work may remain higher-level until evidence and dependencies mature.

## RAID
Track schedule-relevant Risk, Assumption, Issue, and Dependency with an owner and next review/resolution date. Do not create RAID entries that cannot affect the schedule decision.

## Completion
A milestone is not complete because its date arrived. It passes only when its defined acceptance evidence is present.

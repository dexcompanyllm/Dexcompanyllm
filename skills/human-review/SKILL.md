# Skill — Human Review

Human review is a designed control point, not a generic final glance.

Require explicit human decision for high-impact scope such as destructive changes, permissions, secrets, external communication, payment, deployment, legal/compliance judgment, or irreversible business decisions.

Give the reviewer:
- decision required
- evidence
- alternatives
- risk
- recommended option
- exact consequence of approve/reject

Do not ask the user to repeat information already captured.

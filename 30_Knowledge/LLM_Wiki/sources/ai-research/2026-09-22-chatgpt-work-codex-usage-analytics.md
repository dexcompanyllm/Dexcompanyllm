# ChatGPT Work·Codex Usage Analytics

## Confirmed

- OpenAI describes Admin Console analytics that combine usage and cost data, task insights, and outcome metrics across ChatGPT Work and Codex.
- Usage views include active users, credits, and token usage; admins can filter by group or user.
- Insights classify sampled work into use cases/tasks and expose task-level breakdowns such as credits, messages, active users, and model/reasoning/speed distribution.
- Plugin leaderboard and Skills view can support training, ownership, and workflow-maintenance decisions.
- The source describes product guidance and illustrative demo data; it does not prove rollout, eligibility, or visibility for Ethan's account or workspace.

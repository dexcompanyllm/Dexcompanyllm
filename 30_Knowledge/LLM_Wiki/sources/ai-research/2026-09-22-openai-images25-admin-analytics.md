# OpenAI ChatGPT Images 2.5 and Admin Analytics

## Confirmed

- OpenAI announced ChatGPT Images 2.5 on 2026-09-22 Korean time discovery window; the source page is dated September 2026 and states that the feature is rolling out to ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex users across desktop, mobile, and web.
- Reported improvements include more precise editing, better multi-turn consistency, improved preservation of reference subjects, transparent-background handling, and up to 50% lower generation latency compared with Images 2.0.
- ChatGPT-side creative workflow additions include Sketch, templates, image comments, and sharing prompts with an image.
- OpenAI separately described Admin Console analytics for ChatGPT Work and Codex, including usage and credit trends, task/use-case breakdowns, model/reasoning/speed breakdowns, and outcome-oriented analysis.

# OpenAI Ads and Academy updates

## Confirmed

### ChatGPT Ads / Sponsored Agents

- OpenAI announced new ChatGPT Ads experiences on 2026-09-16.
- Sponsored Agents are being tested with select advertisers in the United States.
- Advertisers can use natural-language prompts in ChatGPT Work through the Ads Manager plugin to create, update, and analyze campaigns.
- ChatGPT Ads integrations are available for HubSpot and US-based Shopify merchants; Shopify availability is scheduled to expand internationally where ChatGPT Ads are available from 2026-09-23.
- This announcement does not establish availability for the user's ChatGPT Pro account, Korea, Dex Company, or EarthRadio. Those remain `unknown` unless separately verified.

Source: https://openai.com/index/reimagining-advertising-with-ai/

### OpenAI Academy

- OpenAI announced expanded role-based learning paths on 2026-09-21.
- Relevant paths include Apply AI at Work, Build with AI, and Lead AI Adoption.
- The courses emphasize reusable workflows, checkpoints, human review, and role-specific adoption rather than model-specific configuration.
- Course availability, language, regional access, and any direct ChatGPT Pro entitlement for the user's account are `unknown` unless separately verified.

Source: https://openai.com/index/expanding-openai-academy-with-new-learning-paths/
